Method — and the honest verdict

Win%: margin-aware Elo (K=20, log-margin multiplier, autocorrelation damping), adaptive home-field advantage (EWMA on the home-win residual, tracking the post-2020 decline with no look-ahead), rest-day differential, divisional shrink at prediction time, one-third preseason regression. Ratings are filtered to franchises on the current schedule — relocation ghosts (STL/SD/OAK) are excluded rather than silently displacing real teams.

The measured verdict: the closing market beats this model outright, and in the games where the two disagree the model is right well under half the time — fading the market with this model loses money, measured, not inferred. The live numbers are at the bottom of this tab, recomputed from raw data on every build. So the Edges tab sizes line-shopping only; model win% is never used as a betting signal.

Edges: per-book devig → median no-vig consensus (every book feeds it) → best available price at a book you can actually bet → quarter-Kelly on the price gap, 2% bankroll cap, 3-book minimum. A better number at a book you have no account at is not a better price, so it is disclosed in the "field best" column and never priced off.

Grading: every published probability is logged pre-game and settled against the final score; the Calibration tab is the model's public report card, including the live disagreement record.
